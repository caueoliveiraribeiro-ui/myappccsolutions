# Subscription access decisions

These are agreed product rules for the future subscription implementation.
They do not activate billing or access restrictions.

- Team invitations are included in Personal, Small Business, and Big Business.
- Do not list team invitations on the public homepage plan cards.
- Reports is included in Small Business and inherited by Big Business.
- Reports is not part of the planned Personal entitlement.
- Invitation seat limits and workspace billing rules have not yet been decided.
- Preserve existing user access until the subscription rollout is explicitly approved.
