A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Getting to know your next workspace.

Is Orbit for my business or my personal life?+

Both. Orbit connects personal organization, financial tracking, and business management. Your plan determines which tools you can use, so you can choose the scope that fits your life.

Can I create an account or subscribe today?+

Create your Orbit account, choose a plan, then continue to secure checkout. Your paid features unlock after payment confirmation. You can open the Orbit app.

Can other users see my information?+

Accounts have separate workspaces. Shared access is controlled through invitations and permissions. Only invite people you trust, and choose their access carefully.

Does Orbit buy or sell investments for me?+

No. Orbit helps you record and track stocks and crypto; it is not a broker or trading platform. Market data can be delayed or unavailable. Investment features vary by plan.

Can I work in different currencies?+

The app supports USD, BRL, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, JPY, KRW, MXN, and CHF, with currency preferences and conversion tools. Exchange-rate availability and market coverage can vary. Plan prices on this page are shown in USD.

Can I use Orbit on my phone?+

Yes. Orbit is designed to adapt to phone, tablet, and desktop browsers. Use the same account to access your workspace on your devices.
